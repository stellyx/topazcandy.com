# The Showroom — five service-business website concepts
Published as an isolated subtree of the Topaz Candy site at /showcase/.
This is a portfolio demonstration, NOT a claimed client portfolio.
The parent site's root index.html, comments.html, CNAME, sitemap.xml, robots.txt and assets must stay unchanged.
No navigation is added to the Topaz Candy home page. No GoDaddy/DNS records are changed.
All showcase pages carry noindex,nofollow. The parent sitemap is intentionally unchanged.

## Architecture
- showcase/index.html: independent showroom / site selector
- showcase/{hvac,roofing,landscaping,cleaning,detailing}/index.html: small entry points
- showcase/assets/demo.css: shared component system
- showcase/assets/demo.js: ONE master renderer and five independent business configurations

## Launching a client copy
1. Copy a chosen concept out to its own repository or host. Do not give a customer the parent Topaz repo.
2. Edit the matching configuration in demo.js; set legitimate brand, accurate contact details, licensed service areas, authentic copy, actual job images.
3. Replace the concept ribbon, proof-of-concept warnings and noindex with appropriate real website policies only after verifying details.
4. Connect forms, booking, payment and analytics through authorized providers. Demo forms never transmit or store leads.
5. Audit accessibility, responsive layout, performance, image licensing, SEO, privacy and lead routes; then launch under the customer's domain.
6. Remove unused configs from a client build for true client-level compartmentalization.

## Evidence & asset restrictions
Photos shown are illustrative stock photos from Pexels. Photographer/source pages should be documented before handoff; replace with licensed customer photography when possible.
No reviews, customer counts, guarantees, fake case studies, emergency service promises or client performance metrics are asserted.
All five brands are fictitious examples and are not trading businesses.

## Positioning
Priority customer groups: owner-led HVAC, roofing, landscaping, cleaning and detailing businesses.
Research basis: BrightLocal 2026 mobile/local search study, Tinuiti 2025 home-services study, and ServiceTitan 2025 residential contractors survey.
This is a strategic sales-priority shortlist, NOT a statistically validated "top five industries by website spending."
