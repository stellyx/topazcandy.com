    04 / GOOD TO KNOW

    Before we
    come over.

    A few practical answers. Have something more specific? Add it to your request.

    Ask with your quote ↗
    How long will the carpet take to dry?+

    Drying depends on the cleaning method, carpet, airflow, humidity and amount of moisture used. Your cleaner should confirm an expected drying window and when furniture and foot traffic can return.

    Do I need to move my furniture?+

    Clear small items, breakables and clutter from the areas you want cleaned. Mention larger furniture in your request so moving arrangements and any limits can be agreed before the visit.

    Can every stain be removed?+

    Some marks permanently change carpet fibers or dyes. Tell your cleaner what spilled, how long it has been there and what you have already tried, so realistic expectations can be agreed.

    Can you clean wool rugs or delicate upholstery?+

    Fiber type and the manufacturer’s care label determine the suitable method. Include a photo or label description in your request. Specialist items are assessed and quoted separately.

    Is the price on this page a final quote?+

    No. It is a guide based on the selections you make. This template uses sample rates. A trading business must confirm its service area, room-size rules, treatment needs, taxes and final price before starting work.
