03 / A FEW CLEAR ANSWERS

A little clarity
goes a long way.

What should I include in a service request?+

Describe what you’ve noticed, when it began and which rooms are affected. Include any questions you want to cover and your preferred timing.

Can I plan ahead for a replacement?+

Yes. Choose replacement in the service planner, then note what matters to you—comfort, noise, system age or a future home project.

How does the service planner work?+

It turns your selections into a short brief, right in your browser. Copy it for later. This design study does not book appointments or send requests.
