# TEMPER — home climate concept

Independent HVAC design study at `https://topazcandy.com/showcase/lab/hvac/`.

Warm ivory, forest green and a muted mint accent. Manrope typography, a subdued architectural climate background and a browser-only service planner. This is a fictional brand with illustrative AI media; it does not offer actual HVAC services, book appointments, collect customer details or send inquiries.

## Scope

The new concept lives entirely in this directory. The only shared-page change is adding its fourth card and updating the count in `showcase/lab/index.html`. The original HVAC showroom, root site, other concepts and roofing palette are separate.

## Interaction

- Repair, replacement and seasonal care buttons open the service planner with the matching option selected.
- Timing, concern and optional notes become a sample brief; copy and edit work locally.
- Native modal focus management and Escape closing; labeled choices and visible keyboard focus.
- The hero film fills the background behind the headline and actions, softened with an ivory overlay. A quiet motion control remains available. The video uses a permanent repository asset and static poster, with manual pause/play, reduced-motion and data-saving opt-in, off-screen suspension, hidden-tab suspension and playback error fallback.
- Intentional `noindex,nofollow`; fictional brand and illustrative film disclosure in the footer.
