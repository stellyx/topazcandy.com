A LITTLE HELP AT THE COUNTER

Good questions.

From finding your shape to keeping your design, here are a few things to know before you get carried away.

Can I design my own modular piece?

Yes. Use the design desk to choose a compatible system, swap components and explore colors and artwork. Click a part to edit it. Click empty space or press Escape to return to the whole piece.

Do all the modular parts fit together?

Parts stay within their connection family. The studio keeps compatible choices together and shows a fit summary. A retailer still confirms the actual product variant, finish and final fit before an order.

Can I keep or share a design?

Save it to My shelf in this browser, compare two favorites, copy an exact-build link or download a shop brief from Review this design. A link carries the configuration; it does not place an order.

Are these products available to buy?

This is a Head Shop website demo. The collection uses illustrated sample products, color studies and prices. The bag lets you explore the shopping experience and save a list. It does not take product orders or payments.

What about stock, delivery and custom finishes?

A real retailer confirms available stock, finishes, lead times, shipping and the final quote. The design desk shows catalog references and visual studies to help that conversation.
