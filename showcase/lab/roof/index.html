GOOD QUESTIONS / CLEAR NEXT STEPS

Straight answers.

What happens before a quote?

A conversation about the situation, followed by an appropriate assessment. The contractor should explain what is included in the proposed scope.

What information is useful?

The property, what you have noticed, the approximate date and previous repairs you know about. Use photos you already have or can take from a safe location.

Can I plan before choosing materials?

Yes. Start with priorities and timing, then ask for options, a written scope and a clear project sequence.
