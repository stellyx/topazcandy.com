Imagination script: local outlined Lobster lettering with an original swash, from the OFL-licensed Lobster typeface by Impallari Type. Official source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lobster . The browser loads only SVG and sampled contour data, not the font binary. The nine tattoo motifs are original vector geometry authored for this website. License text is in OFL-Lobster.txt.
