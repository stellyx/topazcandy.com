# $450 website package cart

All six template purchase banners and the homepage/catalog offer open this shared cart. `config.mjs` is the single payment destination for the cart. Template query values are allowlisted and cannot change the package price, currency or payment destination.

The approved offer is $450 USD for one website using a selected design, setup and launch, SEO setup, eligible Google Maps/Business Profile setup or cleanup, business-information and link checks, and three months of website service and maintenance from launch. The included care covers the agreed website and its existing content. AI agents, automation, ongoing AI services, new features, domain registration, paid hosting and third-party subscriptions are agreed separately. Search Console/sitemap/indexing submission is a launch task with owner access; Google controls actual indexing and ranking.

## Current payment and email behavior

The owner requested use of the existing personal PayPal account. The provided QR decoded to `https://www.paypal.com/qrcodes/p2pqrc/ZGLFN3MZMZ2J8`. It does not encode a $450 invoice or automatically track the design/order. The cart displays the amount and tells the buyer to enter $450 USD and choose goods/services if prompted. The email action creates a draft to the verified business mailbox with the selected design, package scope, business details and optional PayPal transaction ID. Visitors review and send it themselves. Contact details remain in page memory; the site has no lead-storage endpoint.

Do not treat opening PayPal, a return URL/query string, clicking an email link, or an entered transaction ID as proof of payment. No automatic payment verification or notifications are installed. PayPal account notification settings and a payment transaction have not been verified. The account was not converted to a business account. No payment was made and no email was sent by this implementation.

A verified existing PayPal.Me link can request an amount for a personal account. Do not guess a handle from a name, mailbox or QR ID. Modern PayPal Payment Links/Buttons require business-account tools. Automated payment receipts need a supported payment integration, server-side verification and an email sender; browser-only success callbacks cannot supply that.
