Deterministic navigation

This calculator traverses only manually validated, directed edges. It does not turn ASCII spacing, map letters, adjacent labels, or raw + counts into guessed movement.

Small tested subset. Galadon revision 5044 says it is slightly outdated. “Market Square → Eastern Road” intentionally returns NEEDS_LOCALIZATION until actual room output anchors Market Square to the map. The conditional geometry is evidence, not an executable route.
