== Notes ==

* If there is a fire in the room, you won't be affected by the freezing cold.
* Freezing cold also gives Chill Touch as well as damage.
* Water containers shatter due to the freezing water, unless in another container (like Backpack, large sack, ect.)

== Credits ==

    To the south lies the Frigid Wastelands, stick to the path, and feel free to
    stop into the watchtowers for rest. Any bandit sightings should be reported
    to the trail guard at once.
