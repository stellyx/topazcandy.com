Temple of Loch Grynmear
Suggested Levels: 1-20

with an inner fire, is the following inscription:

and amazing coding skills that turned my visions into reality, to all

the players of CF.  Without you guys and gals, there'd be no reason for 
the Temple to exist in the first place.  Enjoy!
