                                Shadow Grove      
                                  
Entrance found on the Shadow Grove map. From the edge of shadow grove
look in each direction following "this way is darker", when you reach
'north west east' intersection go west*2, north*3, east.  Looking east
will confirm 'Something different in this direction.'

Norecall after door to Arch-Bishops

F - Fountain                                                                           
S - Draconian spy (key in the robe)
E - Arch-Bishop Merimar ((G)mace of earth, (G)mantle of earth)
F - Arch-Bishop Magmus ((G)orb of fire, (G)mantle of fire)
R - Arch-Bishop Shiamoor ((G)mantle of frost, (G)mace of frost, (G)orb of ice)
P - Pope Medius (staff of Medius, (G)(H)mantle of Medius, (G)orb of Medius)
W - Arch-Bishop Plantumau ((G)mantle of water, (G)mace of water)
X - Arch-Bishop Czavko ((G)mantle of electricity, (G)mace of electricity, (G)orb of electricity)
I - Arch-Bishop Aeristoma ((G)mantle of air, (G)mace of air, (G)orb of air)

Notes
orb of fire - say ferrinshilt - Grants resist heat.
orb of air  - say aeriloshilt - Grants fly.

Elemental blasts may dispel shields. Medius only seems to dispel.
(G): Glowing
(H): Humming
