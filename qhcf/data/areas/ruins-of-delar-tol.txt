X = Sleep trap (look in the corpse and you fall asleep)
L = felar sentry (ivory tail ring, leather hindpaws with bone tipped claws,
                  leather forepaws with bone tipped claws)
F = fire giants (nacreous mask of lizard skin, glistening dagger, 
                 decorated mask of lizard skin)
R = Artifact room (gnome with robe covered in glowing runes)
B = Bazaar
A = Arial guard (wandering) (flail wing braces claws red armor)
K = (invis) Khruvnil (long black robe,&#160;?)
C = dwarven captain
  a dwarven captain is using:
  <worn on body>      a suit of mithril armor
  <worn on legs>      a set of thick mithril leggings
  <worn on feet>      a set of wide mithril boots
  <worn as shield>    a burnished shield
  <wielded>           a wide mithril battle axe

S = Duergar scouts
a duergar scout is using:
<worn around neck>  a darkened mithril necklace
<worn on body>      a chainmail shirt of darkened mithril
<worn on head>      a darkened mithril helm
<worn on legs>      a pair of darkened mithril leggings
<worn on arms>      a pair of darkened mithril sleeves
<worn as shield>    a darkened mithril buckler
<wielded>           a bloody flail of darkened mithril
<wielded>           (Glowing) the broken remains of a flaming sword
<wielded>           a twin-bladed darkened mithril poleaxe
