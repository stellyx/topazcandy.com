Maethien
Suggested Levels: 35-51
Written by Phaelim in 2004 for exclusive use by Carrion Fields LLC. No
unauthorized distribution or copying in whole or part.

E - earth elemental (a crude iron chest plate, a crude iron helm, a pair of crude iron leg plates, a pair of crude iron arm plates)
A - furious magma para-elemental (an obsidian pike)
G - gnomish conjurer (simple gray robes, a small pruning axe)
K - Kiendosi (a malformed iron circlet - head, a knotted leather whip (the good one), a thick stack of stained parchments)
L - Lenais (a stone pendant - if you request it, he gives it to you and tells you its best if you take it away.
                              special properties when placed into a container, but what container?
            a dark staff of crooked walnut - he decides to keep it if requested)
M - Gates of Maethien
P - telescope
T - hearth
X - summoning circle
patrolling conjurer (simple gray robes, a studded leather girth, a long, coiled leather whip, a small round shield of gleaming steel)
a patrolling conjurer (simple gray robes, a pair of studded leather boots, a long staff of polished oak)
