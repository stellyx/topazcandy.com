Outskirts of Galadon
====================

                     -=The Outskirts of Galadon=-
                              by Vassagon
                        for Carrion Fields MUD
                       Copyright 1997, H. Beres

Lying at the edge of the expanding city, the Outskirts of Galadon encompass
the entire city.  With the old trail, it is possible to circumvent the city
by walking around its outskirts.
