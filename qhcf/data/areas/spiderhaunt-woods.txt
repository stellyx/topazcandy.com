N - Ettercap Nest
B - by a stream
f - flying spiders (gossamer wings)
H - The hatchery
P - [hidden, camo, invis, duo, aggro] Phase spider
w - wisper spiders one is visible 15 more will appear when you attack
@ - no-exit rooms, can word from; spawns huge spiders to attack you repeatedly
