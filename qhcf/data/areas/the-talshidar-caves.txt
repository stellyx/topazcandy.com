G - The forge, duergar blacksmith (thick iron collar, pair of thick leather gloves, large forge hammer)
C - dark cell
CX- Zalnul the torturer (black leather jerkin, mask of sparkling ruby and caked blood,
    a pair of black leather leggings, a hot steel poker)
W - warded summoning chamber (Algholu)
B - Alistin (amulet of protection, dark violet robe, black leather belt pouch, topaz encrusted bracelet,
    titanium shod quarterstaff)
A - An alcove (desk with papers)
N - Near the base of a large mountain                                                                                
F - Fulstil (ring set with a carved opal skull, black robe of Sorcery, black leather belt pouch, 
    black adamantite shortsword)
    Pile of corpses (single horned dragon skull, arm bone of a giant)
D - Drelnarst (band of onyx, blood red leather jerkin, black leather leggings, studded black leather sleeves,
    double blade pole-axe)
H - Chamber of the High Priest, Kulsthil (obsidian symbol of Talshidirus, rune covered leather jerkin, two-balled battle 
    flail, pair of black leather leggings)
    a box (jewelled sacrificial dagger)
K - Chamber of death, Kildara (necklace of fire opals, demonic looking mask, thin mithril staff) 
    trophy case (pair of dark green gloves, shield emblazoned with a crimson cross, skull of an elf)
P - Before a strange portal
T - Throne room, Balthashmir (black and white striped heartstone ring, obsidian eye amulet, onyx-bladed spear)

Notes
* If you go all the way to the top after entering from the desert you will plummet to your death.
  And then if you try to retrieve your corpse you will die again.
