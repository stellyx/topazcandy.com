d - Stone debris                      
e - Entrance to Sewers (use ladder to get up and down)
g - Gulguthra (hidden mob - plagues)
j - Janrick (Has invis ring which function as key to other parts of sewers)
l - Ladder on ground                          
m - Meegle the mini-taur
s - spiders (flame blade)
S - Water Snakes (Poisons)
SM- Seantryn Modan
t - Tangle of roots (push east or west to get free)
T - Thieves guild (pass door, duo to get here)
u - Undead rot-rat (deafens and spawn giant rats - good prac mob)
