Coral Palace
============

Trick 1:
2s on ArythOcean
Down to deep in the ocean
South and east until down is 'too dark to see' (using exits)
Down
South until on the ocean floor
East until down
Down to kelp forest
Look for 'You think you see a break in the forest.'

Trick 2:
Talitha tells the group 'all south, all east, and scan for sisters.'
Talitha tells the group 'from sisters, it's u s d to kelp forest.'
Swim around one room above the ocean floor,
until you can swim down to 'The kelp forest'.
Scan: You think you see a break in the forest.

Trick 3:
5e, 9s, all down from the reef just south of coral head.
