Temple of Loch Grynmear
Suggested Levels: 1-20

Etched into the side of the spear, in untarnished silver letters that glow 
with an inner fire, is the following inscription:

Loch Grynmear, copyright 2003 by Kastellyn.  Any use of this area or the 
ideas and concepts from this area on any MUD other than Carrion Fields is 
prohibited without the express written permission of the author.

Special thanks go out to Uller, for his help with the concepts and 
characters, to Sebeok and Valguarnera for their tireless walkthroughs 
and amazing coding skills that turned my visions into reality, to all 
the other Imms who helped with the testing and walkthroughs, to Heather, 
for giving me the time to partake in this crazy habit, and finally, to 
the players of CF.  Without you guys and gals, there'd be no reason for 
the Temple to exist in the first place.  Enjoy!
