Reading the maps

Map lines and literal + coordinates are retained exactly. This verifies transcription, not traversability. Do not infer movement across walls, gaps, crossings, diagonals, area boundaries, doors, or special exits. The separate navigation page currently has zero executable edges and refuses routes without runtime evidence.
