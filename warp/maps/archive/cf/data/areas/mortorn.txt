Mortorn

 On weathered stone the inscription reads:
         Scribed by the sage Nazmorghul, in the year of some
         Lord, 1995. Construction completed 1996, and only
         a small amount of sanity was sacrificed. Useable with
         permission.

 Squinting you can barely make out the line:
         with the minor assistance of Byhakee and Danica,
         two of the best helpers friendship could buy,
