Udgaardian Plains
=================

The Plains of Udgaard was rewritten by Yean for exclusive use by Carrion
Fields, LLC in 2007.  No distribution in any form is permitted without
prior consent.  Thanks to all who aided with this project, especially to
Lyristeon, Rayihn, Ysaloerye and Kastellyn, for their patient mentorship,
advice and detailed reviews, and to Valguarnera for his superior coding.
Suggested Levels: 1-15
 
Udgaard Watchtower written by Valguarnera for Carrion Fields in 2002.  It
is the exclusive property of Carrion Fields, and no permission will be given
to use it elsewhere.
