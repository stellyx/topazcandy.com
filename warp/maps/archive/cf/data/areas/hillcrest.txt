Hillcrest
=========

Hillcrest, the village of the gnomes was written by Zachary P. Jones, and has 
been created for use in Carrion Fields only.
 
Thanks to all of the IMMs who made this possible, especially Poetry, Andaren,
Pico, Vassagon, and Sattanos.
 
A special thanks for Morse and the Troupe, for starting me down this road, and
to Shane Travis for all his hard work.
