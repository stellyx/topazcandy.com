Darsylon
========

This area was written in March of 1997 by Laurella.
The original concept of Darsylon was created by
Jeff Spurlin, and is used with permission.

This area was designed for use by Carrion Fields
only.  Any other use without express written consent
from both the designer and Mr. Spurlin is 
prohibited.

Special thanks go out to all the Immortal Staff of
Carrion Fields, for answering my stupid questions
and such, without which this area wouldn't be possible.

                                                -Laurella
