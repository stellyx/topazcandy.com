Troglodyte Caverns
==================

Roughly etched upon the mountain wall is a message. The message reads as
follows:
In the year of 2003, Lord Beroxxus created these lands for the Carrion
Fields Mud. Use of the Troglodyte Caverns is prohibited without written
consent of the author.

Traveller

= - Door
U#- Entrance to up. Leads to entrance to down of same number. 
D#- Entrance to down. Leads to entrance to up of same number.
S - Trogdolyte Seer
q - Sleeping Quarters
A - Arial Corpse (set of talon guards, set of wing guards)
F - Fungus Grove
E - Elite Guard(s)(aggressive)(x2 guarding the Chief)(Bone mask)
  - Minotaur bodyguard (minotaur gear)
B - Adventurers
C - Trogdolyte Chieftain
R - Refuse Room
