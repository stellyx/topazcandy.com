Velkyn Oloth
============

Velkyn Oloth
Suggested Levels: 20 - 51

It is of black rock worn smooth by countless years.  Someone has
engraved letters to its side:
What once was Dol Guldur, is no more...

Area redone in 2001 by Mayesha.  Original by ShadowDancer.
To be used on Carrion Fields only.

1 - Well
2 - Corpse
K - Kitchen
E - Wood-elven Prisoner (heavy steel manacles)
T - Imprisoned Thief
I - Imprisoned Wood-elf Patroller (heavy steel manacles)
M - Demented Madman
G - Sub-commander of the Guards (black breastplate, black steel helmet, black iron leggings, pair of
       fine black leather boots, black silk cape adorned with a blood-red spider, wide black leather
       belt, black iron broadsword, black iron shortsword)
W - Wilted Corpse (torn green cape embroidered with silver
       thread)
D - Disguised Invoker ((G)diamond dagger named 'Light of Redemption')
    Disguised Paladin (small pendant in the shape of a silver phoenix , (G)fiery mithril sabre named
       'Phoenix Edge')
X - Imprisoned Mercenary (arm bands bearing the insignia of two crossed swords)
P - Imprisoned Paladin
S - Imprisoned Shapeshifter (necklace of wolf fangs)
R - Imprisoned Ranger (wide ring rimmed with razor sharp spikes)
B - Black Mold
A - Sleeping Areas: Elite Guards (some sleeping)
H - High Guard [Assassins X2] (black hooded silk cloak, black studded leather vest, pair of fine black leather boots,
       black iron shortsword)
C - Carmnor, the Commander of the Guards (red-trimmed black adamantite plate boots, (H)smooth ruby bracer engraved with

       boots flowing robe of ethereal black silk)
L - An Infernally Hot Dead-End: A huge magma para-elemental
