A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Start with clear expectations.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. We start with simple instructions and ordinary tasks. Browser and email practice are part of the first lessons.

Is this taught in English or Spanish?

The website, student workbook and opening worksheets are available in both languages. Choose your preferred language when you request enrollment; the teaching language for your group is confirmed before payment.

What does the $450 cover?

Eight in-person two-hour classes over four weeks, the student materials and guided work on your website and automation project. A laptop, domain, website hosting and paid third-party tool plans are separate. We discuss any required tool costs before you commit.

When and where are the classes?

The dates, meeting times and venue for the next group are being arranged. Request enrollment below and we will confirm those details and the cancellation terms before payment.

Will I leave with a fully automatic business?

You work toward a usable website, inquiry capture, acknowledgment, owner alert and lead tracker, then test the full path. Your setup depends on your accounts and tool plans. We also teach how customers find you and which decisions need your review. Sales and income are not guaranteed.

Can I join without a business?

Yes. Build a practice project for an idea you care about. You can use fictional details while learning and replace them with verified information when you are ready.
