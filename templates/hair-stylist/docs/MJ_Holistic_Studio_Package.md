# MJ Holistic Studio website audit and launch package

Prepared October 11, 2026. Product: HAIR-01, Crown & Calm. The business uses the singular name MJ Holistic Studio in its logo and current salon directory.

## Recommended course

Keep MJ's existing Square booking and gift card checkout. Give visitors a clearer introduction, explain the service choices, show trustworthy proof and make booking or a text consultation obvious. Correct conflicting business information before investing in advertising. Build repeat visits into the operational plan after the first booking.

This is a recommendation from the audited site and the sources below. No booking lift, ranking improvement, traffic volume or lost revenue has been measured.

## Evidence from the current site

The homepage was fetched live on October 11 using Firecrawl with maxAge 0 and returned HTTP 200. Its rendered HTML and service menu were examined. A bounded URL inventory covered the first 40 results, mostly retail products. This is a sampled audit, not proof that every possible page was discovered.

| Finding | Observed evidence | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Little first visit orientation | Homepage is primarily a service booking menu, with no main H1 in the rendered HTML | Add a clear salon introduction, service overview and first visit guidance |
| Inconsistent opening hours | Menu says Monday 8 a.m.; footer says Monday through Saturday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.; older directories show different hours | Confirm one actual schedule and correct the site, scheduler and public profiles |
| Conflicting location listings | Current site map points to 14005 U.S. 183, Austin 78717; the Phenix Walden Park directory names Studio 124; Birdeye and an older Fresha record show 10222 Pecan Park Blvd #11 | Confirm arrival address, suite and map pin with MJ, then update stale directories |
| Inconsistent brand labels | Current search title uses Mj hair studio; logo uses MJ Holistic Studio; directory uses MJ Holistic Studio | Use the agreed business name consistently without keyword stuffing |
| Appointment menu contains operational clutter | A Product appointment is $0.01 for 30 minutes; several utility entries are mixed into Other | Decide which entries belong in public booking; retain useful consultations and move internal entries out of view |
| Prices are available but need context | Long menu mixes hair, beauty, consultations and combinations | Group services, keep durations visible and describe when to ask before booking |
| Missing proof in the sampled homepage | No stylist introduction or real client gallery appeared in the fetched homepage; public guest reviews exist elsewhere | Add a verified introduction, approved client work and linked review excerpts |
| Zoom is restricted in current markup | Viewport tags include maximum-scale=1 and user-scalable=no | Use an unrestricted responsive viewport and accessible touch targets |

The hidden source also contains generic contact placeholders. They were hidden and are not reported as visible contact errors. The current public phone and email work as link destinations in the source; delivery or response time has not been tested.

## Research and features that fit MJ

DaySmart's salon website checklist prioritizes prominent booking, readable prices, real salon imagery, stylist profiles, contact details and mobile usability. Square already offers the booking infrastructure plus reminder, calendar, cancellation and client management options. Google recommends complete, accurate business information and lets a business set a preferred booking link. Those are a better fit here than replacing a working scheduler.

Austin comparisons were used for structure, not copied branding. Bella Salon separates color choices into useful service explanations with prices, real photos and booking links. Bee Cave Hair Salon illustrates priced service cards, a first visit consultation path and external booking; some placeholders appeared on that site, so it is a structural reference, not a quality benchmark.

| Priority | Feature | MJ setup |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | Booking visible at every useful decision | Header, hero, service rows, finder, contact panel and phone footer point to the existing menu |
| First | Clear services, price and time | 27 normalized menu entries in six filters, with four standalone service guides |
| First | Help choosing an appointment | New/returning guest finder; color change and extensions invite a conversation |
| First | Low effort contact | Tap to call and prefilled SMS. The visitor decides whether to send |
| First | Accurate arrival information | Current address references are shown with a confirmation note. Conflicting hours are not invented |
| First | Authentic trust | Original MJ logo and two short attributed public review excerpts; no fabricated credentials or review counts |
| Next | Real portfolio and introduction | Request an approved stylist portrait and 8 to 12 approved client photographs with service labels |
| Next | Reminders and cancellation terms | Review available settings in MJ's existing Square plan; confirm policy and terms before enabling changes |
| Next | Rebooking and aftercare | At checkout, agree on the next interval and offer a useful care plan. Use provider features only after configuration is authorized |
| Next | Measurement | Connect an approved analytics account and scheduler reporting; distinguish booking clicks from completed appointments |
| Later | Gift cards and products | Existing gift cards remain one click away. Retail products support aftercare without dominating the first visit funnel |
| Later | Advertising | Test only after information, capacity and completed-booking measurement are reliable |

There is no universally best booking platform for every stylist. MJ is already using Square, so the first recommendation is to keep it. Confirm plan eligibility and current fees before adding premium reminders, waitlists, marketing or other paid features. No new subscription has been purchased.

## The funnel

| Stage | Visitor experience | Operational next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Google, referral or a social post reaches the salon homepage or a relevant service guide | Use accurate business information and an approved booking link |
| Understand | Name, location, service choices, prices and first visit help are easy to find | Keep content consistent with the current booking menu |
| Choose | Returning guest opens booking; a new guest can use the finder or ask about a change | Route questions to MJ, not an unattended demo form |
| Reserve | Guest chooses the matching service and a real time in Square | Scheduler sends its actual confirmation and agreed policies |
| Prepare | Guest receives confirmed details and can bring inspiration or hair history | Configure reminders and any required consultation within the provider |
| Attend | Consultation, chosen service, aftercare discussion | Record actual completed visits and transaction data |
| Return | Agree the next visit and provide a useful follow-up or review request | Use consented provider messaging and avoid invented automated workflows |

The custom site reaches a meaningful next action in one or two taps. Full booking requires the scheduler's own service, time and confirmation steps. Those steps were not shortened, automated or fully exercised in this build. Service-specific links open the menu and do not preselect a service.

## What is delivered

MJ homepage plus four service guides: haircuts and finishing, color and balayage, scalp and hair care, extensions and consultations. The design uses warm ivory, lavender, plum and the existing gold logo. It includes a service filter, guest/goal finder, first visit section, FAQs, review sources, directions, call and SMS paths, gift card link, social metadata and a basic verified business identity schema. Structured hours, ratings and medical claims are omitted.

The reusable Crown & Calm template has its own generic identity and Topaz inquiry path. MJ's logo, phone, email, reviews and local information do not enter the generic output. Its prices are explicitly illustrative. The source includes separate profiles, a build script, menu data, checks and an adaptation guide. The editorial photograph is generated and visibly labeled; it is not presented as MJ's work.

Preview copies stay noindex. Canonical URLs for the final client domain, XML sitemap and redirects are launch configuration, not active changes to MJ's current website. No DNS, booking account, public profile or current-domain changes have been made.

## Proposed MJ package

Internal working price: $795 for the launch package, within Topaz's established $495 to $900 simple website/conversion refresh range. This is proposed pricing for an agreed scope, not a charge, invoice, accepted sale or promise of revenue.

Included: five page adaptation, approved menu and brand setup, connection to existing booking/contact/gift routes, mobile and accessibility review, metadata, final-domain publishing assistance, redirect plan, business information correction list and a handoff session. The source and generated preview already exist. Actual account changes, final-domain launch and any paid services follow their own authorized setup.

Optional follow-on: the established $395 Presence Cleanup scope can cover correcting site and directory consistency once account access, exact listings and the approved details are known. Avoid charging the same deliverable twice. Advertising, automation, photo shoots, domains, subscriptions and ongoing support are separately scoped. No recurring fee is presumed.

## Final launch inputs

1. Confirm the business name, arrival address, Studio 124, actual hours and exact preferred map pin.
2. Confirm the service menu, combinations, what's included and any pricing changes. Decide which utility appointments should be private.
3. Supply an approved stylist introduction and portrait, portfolio images and any social accounts to link.
4. Confirm cancellation, rescheduling, deposit and consultation requirements from the actual booking setup.
5. Choose the final hosting/domain approach. Keep Square booking and gift card URLs reachable if the homepage domain moves.
6. Exercise the full desktop and phone experience, choose service/time without making an unintended reservation, and verify real confirmations through an agreed test booking if requested.
7. Configure canonical URLs, sitemap, redirects, public indexability and approved analytics on the final domain. Update the Google Business Profile's preferred booking link with authorized access.

## Verification and limits

Node syntax and appointment decision checks passed, including ten goal/guest paths and isolation of the template's contact routes. Internal anchors and local assets were checked on all generated pages. Main text color pairs meet 4.5:1 contrast after correcting the muted text color. Image delivery uses an optimized WebP around 188 KB.

Full visual and interactive browser QA is pending because the supported browser-control plugin was unavailable in this session. Responsive layout and touch-target rules were checked in the source. No real booking, SMS, email, payment, analytics event receipt or ranking outcome is asserted.
