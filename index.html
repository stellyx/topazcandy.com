WHAT WE FIX

Good businesses should not look questionable online.

Looks unfinished

Template leftovers and stale copy

Old titles, placeholder text, outdated details, spelling mistakes, stale footer information, and the little signs that make a finished business look unfinished.

Doesn't add up

Conflicting business information

Different locations, business names, phone details, emails, service areas, or descriptions telling customers different stories.

Hard to reach

Weak customer paths

Buried quote buttons, confusing booking paths, dead links, contact friction, and pages that make the next step harder than it needs to be.

Hard to find

Search presentation and structure

Page titles, descriptions, headings, local signals, crawl basics, useful landing pages, and the information search engines use to understand the business.

THE SMALL QUESTIONS

We ask what customers never will.

Most people will not report the red flag. They will just hesitate, back out, or choose the next result.

On a phone

  • Can the important thing be found with one hand?
  • Does the contact action actually work?
  • Does anything look broken, tiny, crowded, or abandoned?

In search

  • Does the title identify the real business?
  • Do location and service signals agree?
  • Are important services represented clearly?

Before they call

  • Does the site answer the obvious trust questions?
  • Do names, numbers, and details match?
  • Is the next step obvious without hunting?
