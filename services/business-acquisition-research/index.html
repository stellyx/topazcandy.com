Business acquisition research

Investigate the market, digital presence, business model, and operational questions before buying.

Discuss this service ↗Back home ↗
Built around your project

Know what needs a closer look.

A business for sale comes with a story. We help examine that story through the available evidence: the market, competitive position, public reputation, digital assets, operating model, and information provided for review.

The result is a research brief with sourced findings, unresolved questions, and a practical due-diligence checklist. We can also map the work needed to improve the web presence, messaging, or operating systems after a purchase.

Where we begin

Tell us the type of business and what you need to understand before proceeding. We will agree on the research scope and available material.

What we can include

  • Market and competitive research
  • Website, search, and digital-asset review
  • Business-model and operational questions
  • Source notes, assumptions, and evidence gaps
  • A diligence question list and improvement options

We agree on deliverables, required access, cost and timing before the work begins.
