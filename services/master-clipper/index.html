MASTER CLIPPER

Turn long videos, podcasts, interviews, and streams into clear, purposeful short-form clips.

Ideas into useful work

Find the moment. Make it land.

Good clipping starts with understanding the source. We look for a complete thought, a strong opening, and a moment that can stand on its own, then edit it for the platform and the audience.

The work can include reframing, pacing, captions, cleanup, branded treatment, titles, hooks, alternate cuts, and platform-ready exports. A clip should preserve the meaning of the original while giving somebody a reason to keep watching.

Where we begin

Send the source material and the platforms you want to reach. We will agree on the number, style, and format of clips.

What we can include

  • Source review and selected moments
  • Short-form edits and platform reframing
  • Captions, pacing, and branded treatment
  • Hooks, titles, and alternate cuts as scoped
  • An organized pack of finished exports

The final deliverables, access, cost, and timeline are agreed around your project.
