Built around your project

Give the project its sound.

Audio sets the tone before a message has finished its first sentence. We develop the sound around the project: an original music direction, a narration, an audio identity, sound effects, or the finishing work that brings the pieces together.

A scope can cover composition, voice direction, recording or generated voice where appropriate, editing, cleanup, mixing, and final exports. We agree on the intended use, creative direction, and deliverables so the audio fits the finished production.

Where we begin

Share the project and the feeling or message the sound needs to carry. We will plan the production and delivery.

What we can include

  • Music or voice creative direction
  • Agreed original music and narration work
  • Sound design or audio branding
  • Editing, cleanup, and mixing
  • Delivery formats and usage details

We agree on deliverables, required access, cost and timing before the work begins.
