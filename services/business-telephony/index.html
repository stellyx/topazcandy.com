Business telephony & AI voice

Automated phone answering, call menus, routing, appointment intake, and follow-up.

Discuss this service ↗Back home ↗
Built around your project

Make the phone part of the system.

Telephony is the word for the phone side of the business: how calls arrive, how they are answered, where they go, and what happens after them. We design that flow around your customers and the people who need to act.

That can mean an automated receptionist, an AI voice assistant, an interactive phone menu, after-hours intake, call routing, or a structured summary for the team. We agree on the language, information, transfer rules, and usage before implementation.

Where we begin

Tell us what should happen when somebody calls. We will work through the conversation and the action behind it.

What we can include

  • Call flow and voice experience design
  • Approved answers and intake questions
  • Routing, transfer, and fallback behavior
  • Connections to appointments or team tasks
  • Test calls and ongoing usage planning

We agree on deliverables, required access, cost and timing before the work begins.
