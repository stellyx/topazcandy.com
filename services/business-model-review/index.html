Business model re-evaluation

Examine the offer, audience, pricing structure, delivery process, and route to market together.

Built around your project

Look at the whole business.

When the business has changed, its model may need a fresh look. We examine who it serves, what it sells, how the offer is packaged, how the work is delivered, and how customers find and choose it.

The review connects market research with practical operations. We document assumptions, identify gaps in the available evidence, compare alternative approaches, and turn the findings into options and next steps you can evaluate.

Where we begin

Bring the business as it is today and the question you are trying to answer. We will define the evidence needed for a useful review.

What we can include

  • Current offer and customer-path mapping
  • Audience, competition, and positioning research
  • Pricing and delivery-model examination
  • Alternative models and documented assumptions
  • A prioritized plan for testing improvements

We agree on deliverables, required access, cost and timing before the work begins.
