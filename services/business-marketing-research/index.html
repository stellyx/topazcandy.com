Business strategy & research

Business & marketing research

Deep research into current and historical business models, markets, audiences, and marketing approaches.

Discuss this service ↗Back home ↗
Built around your project

Find the context behind the next move.

A useful research question can look forward and backward. We study the market as it is, the models that came before it, and the conditions that made particular approaches work or fail.

Research can cover competitors, customers, positioning, distribution, pricing models, historical campaigns, emerging tools, and routes to market. We organize sources, distinguish evidence from assumptions, and condense the findings into a brief that supports a decision.

Where we begin

Bring the question you cannot answer with a surface search. We will define the useful depth, sources, and output.

What we can include

  • A focused research question and plan
  • Current and historical model comparisons
  • Audience, competition, and marketing research
  • Sourced findings and explicit evidence gaps
  • A concise decision brief and next steps

We agree on deliverables, required access, cost and timing before the work begins.
