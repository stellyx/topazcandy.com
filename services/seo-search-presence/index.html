Built around your project

Help people understand what they found.

We check the information customers and search engines see: titles, descriptions, page headings, service content, contact details, links, and crawl basics. The work starts with a specific issue or an opportunity supported by evidence.

Implementation can include clearer service pages, corrected public information, internal links, structured data, and indexing or analytics setup where access is available. We distinguish an actual technical problem from a generic tool warning.

Where we begin

Bring the site and the concern. We will explain what the evidence shows and agree on a useful scope.

What we can include

  • A prioritized review with evidence
  • Search titles, descriptions, and headings
  • Useful page structure and internal links
  • Business-information and schema checks
  • Verification and measurement planning

We agree on deliverables, required access, cost and timing before the work begins.
